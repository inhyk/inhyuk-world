## 심사 메모 (App Review에 남길 말, 영어)

Jelly Tower is a falling-pair color-matching puzzle game designed by a young developer from his handwritten plans.

- Online accounts (nickname + password, no e-mail) are stored on our Cloudflare server (net.seonn.workers.dev) with the game save, nickname friends (mutual accept), 1:1 messages, blocks and reports. "게임 찾기" matches with a random player; match chat and 1:1 messages are filtered on the server (profanity, phone numbers, links, messenger IDs), quick phrases and jelly stickers are sent as numbers only. 🚫 Block and 🚨 Report are in the match chat, 1:1 chat and result screen; reports are reviewed by the parent on an admin page and can suspend the account. Account deletion for online accounts is by request on the support page for now.
- Chat for on-device profiles (made before online accounts, or guests): players can chat only with someone who shares their room code (online battle) or friend code (friends). Online-battle chat goes device-to-device over WebRTC. When both friends are online, friend messages go device-to-device too. Messages, jelly stickers and friend requests for a friend who is offline are held in a small server mailbox (seonn.dev, private Vercel Blob storage) until the friend's game picks them up, then deleted; undelivered mail is deleted after 7 days, and deleting the account deletes its mailbox. Offensive words are masked on both send and receive, phone numbers and e-mail addresses are refused, users can block friends or mute a match, and 🚩 Report copies the recent conversation for contacting us via the support page. Chat can be turned off in 내 정보 → 설정.
- No sign-in is required. Tap "손님으로 하기" (play as guest). "새 계정 만들기" creates an online account. Older on-device profiles exist only on the device, can be moved to an online account, and can be deleted in 내 정보 (Profile) → 계정 지우기 (Delete account).
- First-time players can try 🐣 연습하기 (Practice), a three-step interactive tutorial.
- Online battle (온라인 대전): online accounts tap 🎲 게임 찾기 on two devices (or invite a friend). Guests and on-device profiles connect two devices with a room code over WebRTC (PeerJS): tap 방 만들기 on one and enter the 6-letter code on the other; nothing is stored on our side for room-code games.
- 제작자 모드 (Creator Mode) is a developer test tool from the young designer's plan. Password: 7777777. It unlocks tower floors, level 50, all challenges and all skins on the current profile, and can reset the password of an on-device profile only (not online accounts).
- The "seonn" poster shown after every 5 finished games is an in-house notice for the developer's own free web game site (seonn.dev). Its button opens Safari. There are no third-party ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking.
